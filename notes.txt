Trump Card / Led card rules
- The trump card is revealed at the start of the game, and remains until the game is over
- The led card is the first card played at the start of the trick
- The trump suit has a 3x multiplier, while the led suit has a 2x
- Players must always follow the suit of the led card or play a trump card (if they can, if a player has neither then they can play any card)
